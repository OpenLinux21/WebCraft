The Setup

I am sketching the pond behind my apartment every morning this week.

Gear

  • Sketchbook, A5
  • Two pencils: HB and 2B
  • A folding stool

Steps

  1. Sit facing the water for five quiet minutes.
  2. Block in the horizon and the biggest shapes.
  3. Add shading only after the shapes feel right.
